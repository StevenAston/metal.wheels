What is held fixed

  • History (1900–2025) is replayed from USGS mine production; the sliders change collection and processing only from 2026.
  • Demand follows copper in use per person on an S-curve towards the saturation level, times UN WPP 2024 population (medium variant), held flat after 2100.
  • Ore grade follows the Gerst 2008 lognormal grade–tonnage curve in 100 m depth slices; extraction takes the cheapest copper first, and new deposits are discovered as needed, at most 1 % of what remains undiscovered each year.
  • Energy uses Norgate & Jahanshahi 2010 (75 µm grind, smelting and refining) plus 2.9 MJ/kg per 100 m beyond 497 m (Harmsen 2013). Secondary (scrap) copper energy is not yet included.
  • After a first shortfall, the model still assumes demand is met; the gap is reported, not fed back.
